# Vouby Community Guidelines

**Version 2026-11-11.** Draft: have this reviewed by a lawyer before publishing.

Vouby is a private place to meet people in Kerala. These guidelines keep it respectful and safe. They're part of our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). Breaking them can get content removed or your account banned.

## Be 18+ and be yourself

- **Vouby is for adults only.** If you think someone is under 18, report them with **"Appears under 18"**. We hide that profile from Discover straight away while we review it.
- Use your real first name and real, recent photos of yourself.
- **No fake profiles, impersonation, catfishing or duplicate accounts.** Selfie verification exists so others can trust that you're you.

## Keep it clean

- **No nudity or sexual content** in photos, bios or messages. That includes underwear or swimwear photos that focus on the body, and sexual requests to people who haven't asked for them.
- No violence, weapons, drugs or gore.
- No hate speech, or attacks on anyone's religion, caste, community, gender, sexual orientation, disability or appearance.

## Be respectful

- **No harassment.** If someone doesn't reply, unmatches or blocks you, that's their answer. Don't contact them through other accounts or apps.
- No threats, intimidation, stalking or sharing someone's private information.
- No sharing screenshots or content from a chat without the other person's consent. Screenshots are blocked in the app for this reason.

## No scams or selling

- **Never ask anyone for money**, gifts, recharges, UPI transfers, investment or crypto, for any reason. If someone asks you, report them.
- No advertising, promotions, recruitment, paid services or sex work.
- No links, phone numbers or social media handles on your profile. We block them to protect everyone from spam and scams.

## Stay safe offline

- Get to know someone in the app first. Verified profiles have a blue badge.
- Meet in a public place, tell a friend where you're going, and arrange your own travel.
- Leave if you feel uncomfortable. In an emergency in India, call **112**.

## Reporting

- Tap **⋯ → Report** on a profile or chat. The person isn't told who reported them.
- Your chats are end-to-end encrypted. You can choose to share recent messages from the reported person with our safety team; we explain exactly what's shared before you send.
- Our safety team reviews reports and may:
  - remove content;
  - hide a profile while they review it;
  - restrict features;
  - ban accounts.
- Serious threats or illegal content may be reported to the police.
